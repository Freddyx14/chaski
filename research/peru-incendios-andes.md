# Wildfires in the Peruvian Andes: research brief for Cal Hacks 13.0

> Track: Anthropic "Claude for Public Benefit". Compiled 2026-10-23. Every figure has its source URL inline.
> Where sources disagree (they often do, because COEN, SERFOR satellites and press counts differ), the brief shows each number with its date instead of picking one.
> **Live-tested this session:** the SERFOR ArcGIS REST endpoints in §5 answered real queries on 2026-10-23. Everything else in §5 comes from docs or a status check.

## 1. Fire seasons: 2024, 2025, 2026

### 2024, the record year
- **Deaths and spread.** 20 dead and ~150 injured by 22 Sep 2024, with 361 fires counted since 1 July ([Infobae, 2024-09-22](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2024/09/22/los-incendios-de-peru-dejan-ya-20-muertos-y-5000-hectareas-arrasadas/)). Wikipedia's compilation puts it at 20 dead, 155 injured, 1,876 displaced, and **22 of 24 departments** affected by 15 Sep ([Wikipedia](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incendios_forestales_en_Per%C3%BA_de_2024)).
- **Hardest-hit regions.** Cusco, Cajamarca, **Huancavelica** and Huánuco ([Infobae](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2024/09/22/los-incendios-de-peru-dejan-ya-20-muertos-y-5000-hectareas-arrasadas/)). On 18 Sep a state of emergency was declared in Amazonas, San Martín and Ucayali ([Wikipedia](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incendios_forestales_en_Per%C3%BA_de_2024); [La República](https://larepublica.pe/sociedad/2024/09/16/incendios-forestales-en-peru-2024-en-vivo-hoy-16-de-septiembre-mas-de-20-regiones-afectadas-14-fallecidos-por-crisis-ambiental-mapa-con-focos-de-calor-y-reportes-del-indeci-en-tiempo-real-334720)).
- **Burned area from satellite.** MapBiomas Fuego counts **262,100 newly burned hectares in 2024**, a national record, and 2.6 M net hectares burned over 2013–2024 ([MapBiomas, 2025-10-29](https://mapbiomas.org/blog/2025/10/29/en-el-peru-la-superficie-quemada-en-12-anos-es-diez-veces-mayor-que-la-extension-de-lima-metropolitana-2/)). Of that, the Andes account for ~1 M ha, or 44% ([Mongabay, 2025-12](https://es.mongabay.com/2025/12/incendios-forestales-millones-hectareas-quemadas-peru/)).
- **Huancavelica in 2024.** As of 16–17 Sep:
  - 22 fires, 3rd in the country after Cusco (36) and Cajamarca (24).
  - 86 emergencies since June, with 856 people affected.
  - 1 dead and 3 with third-degree burns.
  - 512.5 ha of crops lost and 802.8 ha of natural cover destroyed.
  - 920 animals injured and 52 killed. 83 homes affected.
  - Districts hit include Ocoyo, Laramarca, Yauli and Huachocolpa.
  - A firefighter said: *"Three fires at the same time, we responded to only one, the nearest."*
  - Source: [Infoandes, 2024-09-17](https://infoandes.pe/archivos/huancavelica-incendios-forestales-dejan-un-muerto-y-cientos-de-hectareas-de-cultivos-arrasados/).
- **Prevention plan gap.** In 2024 there was no active prevention plan, because the previous one expired in 2022. SERFOR's budget had been cut 79% since 2017 ([Wikipedia, citing press](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incendios_forestales_en_Per%C3%BA_de_2024)).

### 2025
- **Huancavelica.** 37 fires in the region between July and September, and the region was under red alert. In October 2025 seven districts reported fires (Anchonga, Pampas, Caja Espíritu, Santo Domingo de Capillas, Huaribamba, Salcabamba, Tintaypuncu), and municipalities and **comuneros** fought them ([Diario Correo](https://diariocorreo.pe/edicion/huancavelica/huancavelica-siete-distritos-reportan-incendios-forestales-y-tintaypuncu-pide-ayuda-noticia/)).
- **Cusco.** 78 fires and 770+ ha by mid-July 2025 ([Infobae, 2025-07-17](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2025/07/17/cusco-en-alerta-roja-78-incendios-forestales-y-mas-de-770-hectareas-afectadas-en-lo-que-va-del-2025/)). Cusco, Áncash, Puno, Ucayali and Pasco were the regions hit hardest ([Infobae, 2025-09-04](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2025/09/04/incendios-forestales-en-peru-cusco-ancash-puno-ucayali-y-pasco-entre-las-regiones-mas-golpeadas-en-2025/)).
- **Long-run total.** SERFOR recorded 97,000+ fires and 3 M+ ha nationally from 2017 to 2025. Cusco alone lost 500,000+ ha, about 7% of its surface ([Infobae, 2026-10-24](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2026/10/24/mas-de-cinco-millones-de-peruanos-viven-en-distritos-con-riesgo-alto-ante-incendios-forestales/)). Our own live query of SERFOR's "Ocurrencia de Incendios Forestales" layer returned **97,376 records** (§5).

## 2. Root causes
- **Human ignition.** **98% of fires are human-caused** (SERFOR/MINAM/SERNANP, via [Wikipedia](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incendios_forestales_en_Per%C3%BA_de_2024) and [Conservamos por Naturaleza](https://www.conservamospornaturaleza.org/noticia/incendios-forestales-en-peru-cuales-son-las-causas/)).
- **Andean practices behind the fires.** Burning pasture to get green regrowth for grazing, *roza y quema* to clear plots on hard-to-reach slopes, and burning stubble after harvest. Some people also believe burning brings rain or fertilizes the soil. No firebreaks are cut and edges are not cleared, so when the fire jumps to a neighbor's land nobody acts ([Conservamos por Naturaleza](https://www.conservamospornaturaleza.org/noticia/incendios-forestales-en-peru-cuales-son-las-causas/)). The fire season coincides with the yearly pre-sowing burn ([El Comercio](https://elcomercio.pe/lima/incendios-forestales-fuego-que-dana-la-tierra-la-peligrosa-tradicion-de-quemar-los-campos-antes-de-sembrar-puede-provocar-incendios-forestales-agricultura-siembra-medio-ambiente-noticia/)).
- **Climate and fuel.**
  - El Niño cuts highland rainfall, and climate change brings long droughts with humidity below 10% ([PUCP, Dr. Ana Sabogal, INTE-PUCP, 2026-10-15](https://puntoedu.pucp.edu.pe/actualidad/incendios-forestales-en-la-sierra-del-peru-el-clima-cambio-las-reglas-del-fuego/)).
  - IGP data cited by CENEPRED shows fires can rise up to 400% in a drought year ([Infobae](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2026/10/24/mas-de-cinco-millones-de-peruanos-viven-en-distritos-con-riesgo-alto-ante-incendios-forestales/)).
  - Invasive *Melinis minutiflora* grass adds fuel ([Mongabay](http://es.mongabay.com/2026/10/peru-impacto-incendios-forestales-desnuda-falta-politica-integral-manejo-fuego/)).
- **Who sets them.** Mostly smallholder farmers and herders inside comunidades campesinas, using fire as a farm tool. Sabogal on punishment: *"The law that blames local populations is wrong... it's very difficult to prove who started the fire"* ([PUCP](https://puntoedu.pucp.edu.pe/actualidad/incendios-forestales-en-la-sierra-del-peru-el-clima-cambio-las-reglas-del-fuego/)).
- **Amazon slope.** Land trafficking and the advance of the farm frontier also drive fires there ([Mongabay](http://es.mongabay.com/2026/10/peru-impacto-incendios-forestales-desnuda-falta-politica-integral-manejo-fuego/)).

## 3. How the response works, and where it breaks
**Who does what**
- **PCM** is the lead of SINAGERD. It runs the Plan Multisectorial (DS 010-2025-PCM) together with MINDEF, MININTER, MIDAGRI, MINAM, MINCUL, MIDIS, **CENEPRED, INDECI, SERNANP, SERFOR, SENAMHI and the Bomberos** ([SIGRID/CENEPRED](https://sigrid.cenepred.gob.pe/sigridv3/documento/18524); [gob.pe PCM](https://www.gob.pe/institucion/pcm/informes-publicaciones/6395418-plan-multisectorial-ante-incendios-forestales-2025-2027)).
- **INDECI/COEN** consolidates emergencies reported by regional COERs and municipal civil-defense secretaries into SINPAD. It publishes "reportes complementarios" ([INDECI](https://portal.indeci.gob.pe/informe/resumen-incendios-forestales/)).
- **SERFOR** runs satellite hotspot detection ("Alerta Incendio Forestal", using VIIRS, MODIS, NOAA and GOES). Alerts go to **regional and local authorities** through a contact network that verifies them in the field. SERFOR reports field confirmation up 70% ([El Búho, 2026-05](https://elbuho.pe/2026/05/peru-nueva-tecnologia-permitira-responder-mas-rapido-a-incendios-forestales/)). **The alerts go to officials, not to communities.**
- **SENAMHI** issues "Avisos de Condiciones Atmosféricas de Incendios Forestales" ([SENAMHI](https://www.senamhi.gob.pe/site/incendio/?p=aviso-incendio)).
- **On the ground.** Volunteer Bomberos (116), police (105), the military with aircraft, and **comuneros themselves**, who are usually first ([SERFOR channels, via El Búho](https://elbuho.pe/2026/05/peru-nueva-tecnologia-permitira-responder-mas-rapido-a-incendios-forestales/); [TV Perú](https://www.tvperu.gob.pe/noticias/nacionales/peru-registra-cero-incendios-forestales-activos-tras-semanas-de-emergencia-informa-el-mindef)).
- **Under the 2025 law, still to be built.** SERFOR is to coordinate prevention brigades with regional and local governments, linked to the National Early Warning Network, RNAT ([Congreso, 2025-09-10](https://comunicaciones.congreso.gob.pe/noticias/aprueban-dictamen-que-propone-una-ley-para-la-prevencion-y-lucha-contra-los-incendios-forestales/)).

**What breaks (with evidence)**
1. **Fires never get registered.** *"Los secretarios técnicos de los municipios no registran los incendios forestales"* (Yésica Martell, COER San Martín). COEN counted 51 fires while SERFOR satellites saw 56 active in 16 regions, so **34 active satellite fires were missing from COEN reports** ([Mongabay, 2026-10-13](https://es.mongabay.com/2026/10/peru-medio-centenar-incendios-forestales-trece-regiones-extienden-territorios-indigenas/)).
2. **The national monitor went dark at peak season.** SERFOR's platform showed "Access denied" from **14 Oct 2026** for about a week. SERFOR: *"We have had some incidents... working to solve it."* On 19 Oct SENAMHI counted 950 heat anomalies in 24 h ([La República, 2026-10-20](https://larepublica.pe/sociedad/2026/10/20/plataforma-de-monitoreo-de-incendio-forestales-de-serfor-permanece-inaccesible-mientras-se-reportan-cientos-de-focos-de-calor-744760)). The ArcGIS REST layers behind it answered our queries on 23 Oct (§5).
3. **Communities fight with nothing.** In Larata (Apurímac) people used tree branches. Bomberos carried water in their helmets. Some areas are about 7 h from the nearest fire company ([Infobae, 2026-10-23](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2026/10/23/el-drama-de-los-incendios-forestales-en-peru-bomberos-usan-sus-cascos-para-trasladar-agua-y-combatir-el-fuego-en-apurimac/); [Diario Sin Fronteras](https://diariosinfronteras.com.pe/2026/10/19/apurimac-incendio-forestal-amenaza-viviendas-y-afecta-la-flora-y-fauna-en-comunidad-de-larata/)). In Puno and Apurímac it was buckets, bottles and wet clothes, *"sin apoyo suficiente de las autoridades locales y nacionales"* ([El Búho, 2026-10-12](https://elbuho.pe/2026/10/con-baldes-y-ropa-mojada-comuneros-enfrentan-incendios-ante-ausencia-del-estado-video/)).
4. **Triage with no tooling.** In Huancavelica 2024: *"Three fires at the same time, we responded to only one, the nearest"* ([Infoandes](https://infoandes.pe/archivos/huancavelica-incendios-forestales-dejan-un-muerto-y-cientos-de-hectareas-de-cultivos-arrasados/)).
5. **No central public information.** The Defensoría flagged the absence of centralized official fire information and of a working information system, and said prevention budget cuts leave farmers uninformed ([Defensoría, Informe 225, 2024](https://www.defensoria.gob.pe/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Informe-Defensorial-n.%C2%B0-225-Incendios-forestales.pdf)).
6. **Fragmented responsibilities, no burn guidance.** There are no guidelines that separate a planned agricultural burn from an uncontrolled fire, and no wildlife protocols ([Mongabay, 2026-10](http://es.mongabay.com/2026/10/peru-impacto-incendios-forestales-desnuda-falta-politica-integral-manejo-fuego/)).
7. **Who dies.** 8 of the 10 dead counted by 13 Oct were aged 70+ ([Mongabay](https://es.mongabay.com/2026/10/peru-medio-centenar-incendios-forestales-trece-regiones-extienden-territorios-indigenas/)). This fits elderly people burning or defending their own plots, but that reading is our inference and not stated in the source.
- **Quechua-language alerts.** I found **no source** documenting Quechua alerts, and no source documenting their absence either. The SENAMHI and SERFOR products I saw are in Spanish. Treat this as a hypothesis to validate with the founder's own community, not as a sourced fact.
- **"Legislators unsure what to do."** No direct quote found. The closest is indigenous leaders asking Congress for an integral fire-management law (next section).

## 4. Legislation and policy
- **Penal Code.** 4–6 years in prison for causing a forest fire, 8–10 years in protected areas. Administrative fines go up to 5,000 UIT. Critics say Peru is *the only country in the region that prohibits fire use* without an integral fire-management framework ([Actualidad Ambiental, 2026-07-14](https://www.actualidadambiental.pe/mas-alla-de-la-prohibicion-las-tareas-pendientes-para-gestionar-incendios-forestales-en-peru/)). Ecuador approved a national integral fire-management strategy in April 2026 (same source).
- **Ley 31973, the "Ley Antiforestal" (Jan 2024).** Critics say it lets landowners convert forest without permits or EIA, which encourages burning. SERNANP acknowledged it worsens fire risk ([Infobae, 2024-09-16](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2024/09/16/ley-antiforestal-sernanp-reconoce-que-polemica-norma-aprobada-por-el-congreso-agrava-riesgos-de-incendios-forestales/); [Wikipedia](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ley_Antiforestal)).
- **Ley 32106 on environmental emergency declarations** (published 7 Aug 2024). It had no reglamento during the 2024 fires ([Infobae, 2024-09-17](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2024/09/17/incendios-forestales-ley-de-declaratoria-de-emergencia-ambiental-no-cuenta-con-reglamento/)).
- **Plan Multisectorial ante Incendios Forestales 2025–2027.** Approved by DS 010-2025-PCM. It covers **764 prioritized districts, 488 at very high risk and 276 at high risk** ([SIGRID](https://sigrid.cenepred.gob.pe/sigridv3/documento/18524); [PDF](https://cdn.www.gob.pe/uploads/document/file/7521879/6395418-plan-multisectorial-ante-incendios-forestales-2025-2027.pdf)). DS 069-2026-PCM updated it for 2026 ([MINAM](https://www.gob.pe/institucion/minam/normas-legales/8114943-0069-2026-pcm)). Its 2025 allocation was S/ 7,464,545 across 8 agencies ([Mongabay](http://es.mongabay.com/2026/10/peru-impacto-incendios-forestales-desnuda-falta-politica-integral-manejo-fuego/)). Critique: it is reporting, training, awareness and brigades, with no prescribed or controlled burning and no pre-emergency coordination ([Actualidad Ambiental](https://www.actualidadambiental.pe/mas-alla-de-la-prohibicion-las-tareas-pendientes-para-gestionar-incendios-forestales-en-peru/)).
- **Law for the Prevention and Fight against Forest Fires.**
  - Passed by the Pleno on **10 Sep 2025** (PL 3636, 6348, 6556 and others): 86–2–11, with the second vote waived.
  - It bans land-use change on burned forest or protection land, has SERFOR coordinate brigades with regional and local governments, and ties early alerts into the RNAT ([Congreso](https://comunicaciones.congreso.gob.pe/noticias/aprueban-dictamen-que-propone-una-ley-para-la-prevencion-y-lucha-contra-los-incendios-forestales/)).
  - ⚠️ **Promulgation and law number NOT verified.** I could not confirm it was published in El Peruano. Check [El Peruano](https://elperuano.pe) before citing it as law.
- **What critics ask for.** A Comprehensive (Integral) Fire Management Law that recognizes indigenous knowledge. Ángel Pedro Valerio, CARE president: *"We need to promote a Comprehensive Fire Management Law that recognizes indigenous knowledge and strengthens territorial preparedness"* ([La República, 2026-10-23](https://larepublica.pe/politica/2026/10/23/incendios-forestales-las-consecuencias-y-la-desatencion-emergencias-hnews-1240091)). CARE's vice president: *"Deberían enviar bomberos forestales"* ([Mongabay](https://es.mongabay.com/2026/10/peru-medio-centenar-incendios-forestales-trece-regiones-extienden-territorios-indigenas/)).

## 6. Existing tools (don't rebuild)
- **SERFOR "Alerta Incendio Forestal" / GEOSERFOR viewer.** Satellite alerts to *authorities*, district rankings, preventive alerts ([El Búho](https://elbuho.pe/2026/05/peru-nueva-tecnologia-permitira-responder-mas-rapido-a-incendios-forestales/); [GeoSerfor](https://repositorio.serfor.gob.pe/handle/SERFOR/709)). **Gaps:** it is official-facing, the web viewer went down 14 Oct 2026, it is in Spanish, and nothing is pushed to the comunero.
- **Alerta SERFOR app and WhatsApp 947 588 269.** Citizens report illegal burning and wildlife cases, which is a reporting inbox and not an alert ([gob.pe](https://www.gob.pe/87537-whatsapp-alerta-serfor); [repo](https://repositorio.serfor.gob.pe/handle/SERFOR/610)).
- **INDECI SINPAD/COEN.** Depends on municipal secretaries registering fires, which they often don't (§3).
- **SENAMHI avisos.** Regional, weather-only, published on a website.
- **MapBiomas, Global Forest Watch, FIRMS.** Global or analytic tools for after the fact, not community-facing.
- **The gap none of them fill.** A last-mile channel that reaches the *comunidad campesina* (WhatsApp, voice or SMS, in **Quechua**), and that turns a community report into a registered SINPAD-style incident so it stops depending on the municipal secretary. It would also give a burn-day "is it safe to burn today" check from SENAMHI conditions. Feedback channel: community confirmations would mark satellite alerts as true or false positives, which is the same "field confirmation" SERFOR says it struggles to get.

## Alternative: Ica water scarcity (and why it connects to Huancavelica)
- **Asparagus boom.** Asparagus area grew about 25× between 1991 and 2011, from 411 to 10,400 ha. In 2009 the Ica aquifer was over-exploited by 145 hm³, with 335 hm³ extracted against an exploitable reserve of 190 hm³/yr ([summary of Dialnet / Espacio y Desarrollo, via search](https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/10498463.pdf). This figure came from a search snippet and should be re-verified in the PDF).
- **Illegal wells, thirsty crops.** In 2013, **615 of 815** wells in the Ica valley and **all 442** in Villacurí broke the rules. Asparagus uses ~15,000 m³/ha and grapes ~10,000 m³/ha, and the two together covered ~20,000 ha by 2014 ([Damonte et al., Redalyc](https://www.redalyc.org/journal/886/88649163005/html/)).
- **Falling water tables.** Ica, Villacurí and Lanchas aquifers drop by up to ~1.5 m a year despite bans ([Diario UNO, 2026-05-04](https://diariouno.pe/2026/05/04/ica-se-queda-sin-agua-la-sobreexplotacion-agroindustrial-vacia-los-acuiferos-y-condena-a-comunidades-enteras-a-la-sed/)).
- **Link to Huancavelica.** There is a documented **Ica–Huancavelica water conflict**: Ica takes water from Huancavelica's highland headwaters ([DESCO PDF](https://www.desco.org.pe/recursos/site/files/CONTENIDO/1118/10_Salazar_PHj17.pdf). The PDF returned 403 so I did not read its details). The founder has lived in both places, which makes it a strong personal story. Wildfires still have the better weekend data (live APIs).
