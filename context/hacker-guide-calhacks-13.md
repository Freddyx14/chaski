Introduction

Welcome!

2

We’re thrilled to have you join us at Cal Hacks 13.0 this weekend! All of us are super
excited to kick off our thirteenth iteration of Cal Hacks, and you all are the driving force
behind our event. We want to do everything we can to help you navigate Cal Hacks; with
that said, please be sure to read through the entire guide!

This year, Cal Hacks is taking place in San Francisco at the Palace of Fine Arts! In this
guide, you’ll ﬁnd resources for navigating the venue and surrounding area, as well as an
itinerary of the workshops, ceremonies, and events we’ll be hosting!

Whether Cal Hacks is your ﬁrst hackathon or your ﬁftieth, what we believe makes
hackathons so valuable are the endless opportunities to learn. Cal Hacks wouldn’t be
possible without the support from our community, from our incredible sponsors and
mentors to our volunteers from across the nation. With their support, we’re excited to
provide you with the resources you need to build out your projects from ideation to
reality. We encourage you to check out our workshops, brought to you by our sponsors
and Berkeley student organizations, and to reach out to our mentors — there’s no better
way to discover new ways to build!

What truly makes Cal Hacks special, though, is you. Each hacker brings a wealth of
unique experiences and ideas; it’s your curiosity, creativity, and love for building that
drives us to put on this event. Whether you come to San Francisco with a team or
discover one when you arrive, the best ideas are formed from creative minds coming
together to build something incredible. The projects that come out of Cal Hacks never
fail to amaze; you inspire us every day to make Cal Hacks the best it can possibly be.
Thank you so much for coming — we can’t wait to see what you build.

Happy hacking!

Sincerely,
The Hackathons @ Berkeley Team <3

Alternate Parking & Transportation

Public Transit

The venue is easily reachable by public transit! If you’re traveling from outside San
Francisco, we recommend taking the BART into the city and taking a Muni or Golden
Gate Transit bus to the venue.

Rideshare

Due to the large number of attendees, and limited roads leading into the Palace, we
recommend getting dropped off a 5-10 minute walk away. There will be no drop-off zones
near the venue otherwise.

Driving

There will not be hacker parking at the venue. There will be a very limited number of
public parking spaces available at the Presidio Lot, but most spaces will be reserved for
sponsors. We reserve the right to tow away any unauthorized vehicles on the property, so
please plan to park at a city lot nearby if you plan on driving.

Registration & Team Formation

➔  Every hacker must be registered to participate!

➔  No team? No problem! Hop into the #1-team-formation channel on Slack, use the

event after the Opening Ceremony!

➔  Make sure to add your teammates on Devpost during submission. No other

conﬁrmation is required to formally create a team.

Project Submission & Judging

All completed hacker projects must be submitted on Devpost BY 9:00 AM, on Sunday
morning. Late projects will not be considered for judging, and you MUST be
in-person at the venue for the judging period to be eligible for judging. Shortly after
the 9:00 AM submission deadline, all projects will be assigned a table number. You will
need to move to your assigned table by 10:30 AM in order to be judged. Each table will be
shared by 2-3 teams.

All projects must be started during the event. Sadly, we disqualify a number of entries
every year for violating this rule. Your project should also follow the MLH Code of
Conduct.

We highly recommend that you submit an initial draft of your project on Devpost by
Saturday night, and continue editing your submission until the deadline, to avoid
missing the deadline.

Judges will visit project teams between 10:30am to 12:30pm; this will be your chance to
show off the amazing projects you’ve built! You must stay at your table for the full
judging window! If your entire team is not present while a judge comes, you will not
be judged!  We recommend preparing a two-minute pitch and any answers to possible
questions! Hacks will be judged on a variety of factors, including impact, quality,

If there is an emergency or immediate threat to life, call 911.

➔  Do I need to show up for all 3 days?

While the Cal Hacks experience is incomplete without attending the entirety of
the event, you can arrive late or leave early if you have other commitments.

➔  I have a dietary restriction. Will Cal Hacks be able to accommodate?

We will be offering vegetarian options as a part of our meals! Due to the vast
variety of dietary restrictions, however, we are unable to completely account for
speciﬁc allergies or restrictions. For non-vegetarian hackers, we ask that you avoid
grabbing vegetarian meals if possible to ensure vegetarian hackers are able to eat!
Please refer to our food menu for detailed allergen information. If you have
concerns about allergies/GF please reach out to a director and we can assist.

➔  Are hackers required to stay overnight?

Nope! Some of our hackers are returning to Berkeley for the night, or staying in
different nearby hotels. Though staying overnight is a hackathon tradition, do
whatever you feel most comfortable with! Our sleeping areas will be divided into a
men’s and women’s sleeping area to provide hackers more privacy.

It wouldn’t be Cal Hacks without him. He’s grown a lot since you last saw him!
