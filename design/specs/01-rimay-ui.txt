RIMAY — iOS APP UI, FIRST PASS. SIX SCREENS ON ONE BOARD.

WHAT THIS IS
Rimay is a pocket interpreter between Spanish and Chanka Quechua for the staff of a rural health post in Pilpichaca, Huancavelica, Peru (3,800+ m in the Andes). The nurse holds an iPhone between herself and the patient during the consultation. She speaks Spanish; Rimay says it aloud in Quechua. The patient answers in Quechua; Rimay detects the language by itself and tells the nurse in Spanish what the patient meant. Before Rimay says anything, it audits its own translation: if a dose, a time, a number of days or a follow-up date changed, it refuses to say it and shows the nurse exactly what to check. The avatar is Chaski, a young vicuña wearing an interpreter headset and a teal scarf (images attached).

WHO USES IT AND WHY THAT DECIDES THE DESIGN
The main user is the nurse, mid-consultation, with a patient in front of her. She glances at the phone for one or two seconds at a time. So: one primary action per screen, huge tap targets, the current state readable from across a desk, and the safety information (what was audited, what was blocked) impossible to miss. The patient may not read at all, so the patient-facing side is the voice and Chaski, never small text. This is a medical tool used in a poor rural setting: calm, warm, trustworthy, never playful at the cost of clarity. Chaski brings the warmth; the layout brings the seriousness.

CANVAS
Six iPhone screens, each 402 x 874 pt, laid out left to right in one row with 80 pt gaps, on a board background #ECEFEC. Label each screen above its frame in Inter 600 18 pt #5B6A65: "1 · Consulta lista", "2 · Escuchando a la enfermera", "3 · Rimay habla en quechua", "4 · El paciente responde", "5 · Rimay no lo dijo", "6 · ¿Me lo repite?". Each screen has the iOS status bar (time 9:41, 54 pt tall area) and a home indicator (134 x 5 pt, radius 3, #18221F at 100%) 8 pt above the bottom edge.

TYPE
Inter only (SF Pro is not available; Inter is the stand-in). Display: Inter 700. Body: Inter 400/500. Numbers in Inter with tabular figures. Scale: 34 / 24 / 20 / 17 / 15 / 13 pt. Line height 1.3 for body. Quechua text is always set one step LARGER than its Spanish companion, because it is what the patient hears and what the nurse needs to see was said.

COLOR (exact)
- Screen background #F6F4F1 (warm off-white, matches Chaski's world)
- Surface cards #FFFFFF, 1 pt border #E4DED8, radius 20
- Ink #18221F, secondary ink #5B6A65, tertiary #8A948F
- Primary teal #0E6B64 (Chaski's scarf). Primary pressed #0A524D. Teal tint #DCEEEB
- Cinnamon accent #C9763A (Chaski's fur), used ONLY for the Quechua language tag
- Audit OK green #1E7A3E on #DDF2E3
- Warning amber #9A5B00 on #FBEBD0
- Blocked red #B02344 on #F8DDE4
No gradients anywhere EXCEPT the Chaski card (see below). No purple. No blurred shadows: depth comes from 1 pt borders only.

CHASKI CARD (appears on every screen)
Chaski's images have a vertical gradient background from #C5BAB1 at the top to #E2DBD5 at the bottom. Place each Chaski image inside a rounded card whose fill is EXACTLY that linear gradient (top #C5BAB1, bottom #E2DBD5), radius 28, so the image's square edges disappear into the card. Never put Chaski on white directly. Use the image that matches the state of the screen (named per screen below). Chaski card is 240 x 240 pt on screens 1, 3 and 5 (Chaski is the protagonist), and 112 x 112 pt on screens 2, 4 and 6 (Chaski is secondary).

LANGUAGE TAGS
Pill, height 28, radius 14, Inter 600 13 pt, 12 pt horizontal padding. "Castellano" = #18221F text on #ECEFEC. "Quechua chanka" = #FFFFFF text on #C9763A. They tell the nurse at a glance which language is on screen.

TOP BAR (every screen)
Below the status bar: left, patient chip "Don Teodoro · presión alta" (Inter 500 15 pt #18221F, a 28 pt circle avatar with initials "DT" in #0E6B64 on #DCEEEB). Right, a 36 pt circular button with a document icon (#5B6A65) for the visit summary. 16 pt side margins on all screens.

TASTE AND BANS
Calm clinical warmth, inspired by the best iOS health apps, with Chaski as the only illustration. No emoji in the UI. No stock icons grids. No lorem ipsum: use the copy above verbatim. No gradients except the Chaski card. No drop shadows. Teal is the only action color; red appears only on screen 5 and the one failed row on screen 6. Keep generous whitespace but no empty gap larger than 48 pt above a primary button.
